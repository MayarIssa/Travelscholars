# Travel Scholars

My shortlist of master's programs in AI, Computer Science and Data Science for 2027:

- Germany: public universities with no tuition that accept an MOI letter
- USA and Canada: funded programs with a stipend that accept an MOI letter
- Worldwide: fully funded scholarships with MOI or no English test

Three profiles (Mayar, Rodayna and Aya; Aya has her own program list in data-aya.js), each with its own checklist, application status, notes and optional PIN. Each program shows its world and national ranking (THE World University Rankings 2027), deadlines, requirements, documents, what I pay the university, the stipend, the money to bring from Egypt and the overall cost.

Static site (HTML, CSS, JavaScript). Data lives in `data.js`.
